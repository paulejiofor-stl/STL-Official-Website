Our story

From one critical mandate to a full-service partner.

Standpoint began in 2015, supporting SIM registration and KYC devices for Airtel — work where accuracy, uptime and regulatory compliance are non-negotiable.

That foundation shaped how we operate. Over the past decade we have grown into a full-service technology company spanning IT infrastructure support, managed services, technology sourcing, identity solutions and, most recently, AI adoption.

Our aim has never changed: deliver exceptional service at fair value, and earn the right to be a long-term partner. Today we support some of Nigeria’s most recognized telecoms, financial services and enterprise brands, with coverage across all 36 states.
