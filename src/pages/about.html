Our story

From one critical mandate to a full-service partner.

Standpoint began in 2015 with a simple mandate: support telecom operators with reliable SIM registration and KYC device operations. We started with Airtel, delivering field support across Lagos, the West and the North. In this work, accuracy, uptime and compliance are non-negotiable.

That foundation shaped how we operate. Over the past decade we have grown from a single field operation into a nationwide technology partner spanning IT infrastructure support, managed services, device lifecycle management, technology sourcing and, most recently, AI enablement.

Our aim has never changed: deliver exceptional service at fair value, and earn the right to be a long-term partner. Today we support some of Nigeria’s most recognized telecoms, financial services and enterprise brands, with coverage across all 36 states.
