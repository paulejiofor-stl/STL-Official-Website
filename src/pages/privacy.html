1. Who we are

Standpoint Technology Limited (“Standpoint”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal data collected through this website, through enquiries made to us, and through our ServicePoint device repair service.

Contact: Block A2-6, Suite 254, Sura Complex, Simpson Street, Lagos Island, Lagos, Nigeria · info@standpointng.com · +234 201 454 4012

This policy explains how we handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission’s General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect only what we need. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Enquiries: your name, company, job role, email address, phone number and the content of your message when you contact us by email, phone or through our contact form.
  • Clients and suppliers: business contact details of the people we work with, and information needed to deliver, invoice and support our services.
  • ServicePoint customers: your name, phone number, email, pickup and delivery address, device details (such as make, model and serial number), a description of the fault, and payment records.
  • Website visits: technical information our hosting provider processes automatically to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric, religious or political information) through this website. Please do not include it in messages to us.

Data on devices you send us. Devices handed to us for repair or support may contain personal files. Our technicians access a device only as far as needed to diagnose and fix it, and do not copy, view or keep personal content beyond that. We recommend backing up your data before handing over any device.

3. How and why we use your data

The NDPA requires a lawful basis for each use of personal data. We rely on the following:

PurposeLawful basis
Responding to your enquiry and preparing quotes or proposalsSteps you have asked us to take before entering a contract; our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries
Delivering services, repairs, pickups and deliveries, and providing supportPerformance of a contract with you or your organisation
Invoicing, accounting, tax and regulatory record-keepingCompliance with legal obligations
Keeping our website, systems and client environments secureLegitimate interests, balanced against your rights
Sending you news or marketing about our servicesYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing or profiling.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where necessary, and only with parties bound to protect it:

  • Service providers who act on our behalf, such as website hosting and security (Cloudflare), email and productivity platforms, logistics partners for device pickup and delivery, and payment providers.
  • Technology partners and manufacturers (OEMs) where needed to process warranty claims, order parts or escalate technical issues.
  • Professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law, regulation or court order.

Where a provider processes data for us, we put written terms in place requiring them to use it only on our instructions and to keep it secure.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers (for example, hosting and email platforms) may store or process data outside Nigeria. When this happens, we transfer data only where the NDPA allows it — for example, where the recipient provides an adequate level of protection through law, binding corporate rules, contractual clauses or certification, or where another lawful condition under the Act applies — and we keep a record of these transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law. In practice:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are deleted when no longer needed to respond or follow up.
  • Client, supplier and ServicePoint records are kept for the duration of the relationship and afterwards for as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes.
  • Marketing preferences are kept until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it securely or anonymise it.

7. How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encryption in transit, secure configuration of our systems and staff confidentiality obligations. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and inform you directly without delay where the risk to you is high.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Access — confirm whether we process your data and receive a copy of it.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate, outdated or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure — have your data deleted where it is no longer needed or there is no lawful basis to keep it.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data while a request or objection is being resolved.
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it. This does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another organisation where technically feasible.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (see section 12).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@standpointng.com with the subject “Data protection request”. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request, and we will respond without undue delay.

9. Cookies

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and our fonts are hosted on our own servers. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the site from malicious traffic; these do not identify you and do not require consent.

If we introduce non-essential cookies in future (for example, analytics), we will ask for your consent first through a clear accept-or-reject choice, and update this policy.

10. Children

Our website and business services are intended for adults and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Where a ServicePoint repair involves a child’s device, we deal only with a parent or guardian.
